About & help

What this is, how it works

About Lyric Studio

Lyric Studio is a quiet, focused workspace for song lyrics and poetry. The editor is the whole room — a single page where your draft lives — and everything else (context, tools, analysis) stays out of the way until you open it.

It pairs a clean writing surface with a stack of AI-assisted tools for sound, revision, and imagery, plus lightweight local analysis for rhythm and structure. It’s open source, free, and your drafts stay in your browser.

Made for writers who want the page first and the gadgetry second.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get AI help on a line?

Select a phrase in the editor, or just leave the cursor on a line. Open Tools, pick a tool (rewrite, imagery, metaphor, rhyme…), and run it — it uses your selection first, and falls back to the active line.

What does the genre picker do?

It shapes every AI suggestion — vocabulary, conventions, and structure shift to match the genre you choose. Set it in the Tools panel before running a tool.

How do sections and rhyme schemes work?

Add section labels on their own lines — [Verse], [Chorus], [Pre-Chorus], [Bridge], [Intro], [Outro], [Hook], [Interlude], [Solo], and many more. Any capitalization works, numbers and letters work ([Verse 2], [Verse II], [Verse A]), shorthands work ([V2], [PC], [M8]), and repeat markers work ([Chorus ×2]). The Analysis dock then reads the draft by part: per-section line and syllable counts, the song’s flow, and per-stanza rhyme schemes like AABB.

Can the AI write the whole thing for me?

The AI can generate suggestions for lines and stanzas, but it won't write the entire song/poem for you. You can copy and paste its suggestions into your draft as needed. This is to encourage users to add their own soul and creative input.

Are my drafts saved anywhere?

Yes, locally. Your draft is auto-backed-up in your browser for an hour, and tshe Context panel keeps a small history of versions you can restore. Nothing is sent to a server except the text you explicitly send to an AI tool.

Do I need an API key to use this?

The AI tools need an OPENAI_API_KEY on the server to work with the full model. Without one, tools still run — they fall back to lightweight browser-side versions so you can keep writing.

Is Lyric Studio open source?

Yes — it’s Apache-2.0 licensed and hosted on GitHub. You can read the source, report issues, or contribute at github.com/HBIDamian/LyricStudio.

How can I support the project?

If Lyric Studio is useful to you, you can help keep it going via ko-fi.com/hbidamian.
